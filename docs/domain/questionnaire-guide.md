1. Name and Residence 
**Purpose** List formal names and current addresses and personal identifying information for the voluntary petition and statement of social security number form

**Required fields:**
A. Your Full Name
   Your Spouse's full name (only if married)
B.  Your Social Security Number
     Your Spouse's Social Security number (only if married)

C. Your Date of Birth
   Your Spouses date of birth (only if married)

D. Any other names used by you or your spouse in last 8 years.

E. Current Address

If you are leasing (If applicable)
Name of landlord or management company
Adress of landlord or agency Terms

F. Telephone Number and E-mail

G. Prior addresses in the last 3 years prior to filing

**Only need spouse information if married**. 

**Contact information** Phone number(s) and E-mails(s) needed for internal use but not for petition.  Date of birth is also not listed on petition but this combined with other information is used to obtain credit report.  

**Social Security Numbers** used for statement of social security numbers form

**Landlord information** Used for schedule G - executory contracts that need to be assumed or rejected.  

**Common Mistakes** 
- Clients often don't list current lease terms correctly per section E. 
- Clients often don't list all addresses in last 3 years per section G.  

**Edge Cases**
-Only list spouse information if married or make that required if married
